Compás — navigation map

Every state the app can be in, and every way from one to another. Drawn from the text file docs/product/navigation.md, which is the reference to edit. 215 states (215 drawn as screens, 0 planned) and 835 transitions. 52 of them happen without a tap (a full phone, a ringing call, an empty camera roll); in the prototype they are offered under "What can happen here". 0 are not built yet and 0 lead to a stand-in. Open any state in the prototype from the list under its flow.

Checks: all pass. Every state can be entered and left, every reference exists, and every designed state can be reached with what is built today.
How to read it

Each flow is drawn as a few boxes. A box is a group of states that belong together, with their IDs underneath; the full list of states and of transitions is under each diagram. A blue arrow is the main path, a dashed arrow happens without a tap, a grey box is another flow. Going back and the tab bar (Library, Teachers, Settings, Search) are left out of the drawings.

The same states and transitions as Mermaid text, one box per state, are in docs/product/navigation-map.md. The words, not the drawings, are the reference: docs/product/navigation.md.

Overview: how the flows connect

A dancer films or imports a move, studies it in the player, and keeps teachers, steps and settings around it. The library, search, teachers and settings are the four tabs, reached from the tab bar on every main page.

The 14 flows are grouped into nine boxes. 12 of the 28 connections between the boxes are drawn; each flow below lists every flow it comes from and goes to.
