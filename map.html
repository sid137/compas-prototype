How to read it
White box, solid outlineA state that is designed.
Grey box, dashed outlineA state that is planned and not drawn yet. There are none at the moment.
Blue rounded boxA state in another flow that this flow comes from or goes to.
Plain arrowOpens the new state on top. Back returns.
Thick arrow, marked ⇄Switches: replaces the current state (tabs, on and off).
Arrow marked ⌂Finishes a task and clears the trail, so Back goes to the Library or the tab root.
Arrow marked ⏱Happens by itself.
Arrow marked ≈A stand-in: the prototype goes there, but it is the wrong place. The right state is not drawn yet.
Arrow marked ◇Happens because of something other than a tap (a full phone, an empty camera roll, turning the phone). The state is drawn, and no tap leads there; in the prototype, pick it under "What can happen here" in the list of screens.
Dotted arrowA transition that is planned and not built yet.
"←" line inside a boxThe controls on that state that go back to wherever the person came from.
The tab bar (Library, Teachers, Settings, Search) is on every tab root and is left out of the diagrams to keep them readable.
